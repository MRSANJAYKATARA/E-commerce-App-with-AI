## 2. Key Definitions (as per DPDP Act, 2023)

- **"Data Fiduciary"** (Section 2(i)): Means ExamLegacy / SANJAYXLEGACY, who alone determines the purpose and means of the processing of personal data.
- **"Data Principal"** (Section 2(j)): Means the individual to whom the personal data relates (the student, subscriber, or user). Where the individual is a child (below eighteen years of age), it includes the parents or lawful guardian.
- **"Digital Personal Data"** (Section 2(n)): Means personal data in digital form that identifies or is capable of identifying a Data Principal.
- **"Data Processor"** (Section 2(k)): Means any authorized service entity (e.g., Google Firebase, Cashfree Payments) that processes personal data on our behalf.
- **"Consent"** (Section 6): Means free, specific, informed, unconditional, and unambiguous indication of the Data Principal's wishes through clear affirmative action.
- **"Board"**: Refers to the Data Protection Board of India established under Section 18 of the DPDP Act.

## 8. Technical and Organizational Security Safeguards (Section 8(5))

ExamLegacy enforces robust, enterprise-grade safeguards to prevent unauthorized access, loss, or personal data breach:
- **Encryption:** All data in transit is encrypted using Transport Layer Security (TLS 1.3 / HTTPS).
- **Access Isolation:** Web server rules strictly deny direct HTTP access to configuration files, database migrations, and private storage directories (`403 Forbidden`).
- **Cryptographic Access Control:** Ephemeral short-lived streaming sessions prevent direct hot-linking or unauthorized downloading.
- **Database Concurrency:** All financial wallet transactions utilize database-level isolation (`SELECT ... FOR UPDATE`) to guarantee ledger immutability and prevent race conditions.
- **Personal Data Breach Protocol (Section 8(6)):** In the unlikely event of an unauthorized personal data breach, ExamLegacy will notify the **Data Protection Board of India** and affected Data Principals promptly, specifying the nature of the breach, affected data categories, and immediate remedial measures taken.

## 9. Statutory Rights of the Data Principal (Sections 11, 12, 13, 14)

Under the DPDP Act, 2023, you are endowed with enforceable statutory rights:

### 1. Right to Access Information (Section 11)
You have the right to obtain a summary of your personal data being processed by us, a description of the processing activities, and the identities of all third-party Data Processors with whom your data has been shared.

### 2. Right to Correction and Erasure (Section 12)
- **Correction:** You may update or correct inaccurate, outdated, or misleading profile information via the **Account** tab or by writing to our Grievance Officer.
- **Erasure / Account Deletion:** You have the right to request erasure of your personal data when it is no longer necessary for the purpose for which it was collected. *(Note: Transactional receipts and financial ledgers will be retained in accordance with statutory accounting obligations under Indian tax laws).*

### 3. Right of Grievance Redressal (Section 13)
You have the right to readily accessible grievance redressal regarding any act or omission by ExamLegacy concerning your personal data.

### 4. Right to Nominate (Section 14)
You have the right to nominate any other individual who, in the event of your death or incapacity, shall exercise your rights as a Data Principal under the DPDP Act.
