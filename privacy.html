1. Statutory Notice & Identity of Data Fiduciary

This Privacy Policy constitutes the official statutory Notice issued under Section 5 of the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 by ExamLegacy (Data Fiduciary), operated and powered by SANJAYXLEGACY.

ExamLegacy provides digital competitive exam materials, high-yield study notes, in-app encrypted PDF readers, and AI learning accelerators for students across India (NEET, JEE, UPSC, SSC, Banking, and University exams). 2. Categories of Personal Data Collected (Data Minimization)

In accordance with Section 8(1) of the DPDP Act, we practice strict data minimization: we collect and process only the minimal personal data required to administer your learner account and deliver our educational services:

3. Protection of Children’s Personal Data (Section 9)

ExamLegacy recognizes that students preparing for competitive examinations may be below eighteen (18) years of age:

  • Parental / Guardian Consent: Individuals under 18 years of age must use ExamLegacy under the active guidance and consent of a parent or legal guardian.
  • No Detrimental Processing: We do not engage in any processing of personal data that could cause detrimental effects on the health, education, or well-being of a child.
  • Zero Behavioral Tracking & Ads: We strictly prohibit tracking, behavioral monitoring, or targeted advertisements directed at minors. ExamLegacy is completely free of third-party advertisements.

4. Digital PDF Content Protection & Dynamic Watermarking

All digital study PDFs, formula handbooks, and revision modules are proprietary intellectual property.

  • Secure Streaming: Purchased PDFs are never exposed as public downloadable files on the web. They are delivered via short-lived encrypted streaming sessions into an ephemeral in-memory viewer.
  • Personalized Watermarking: Every rendered page features a transparent digital security watermark displaying your registered student details and license timestamp. This processing is performed strictly for intellectual property protection and anti-piracy deterrence under Section 7 of the DPDP Act.

5. Enforceable Statutory Rights of the Data Principal

Under Chapter III (Sections 11–14) of the DPDP Act, 2023, you enjoy statutory rights regarding your personal data:

  • Right to Access Information (Section 11): Right to obtain a summary of your personal data processed by ExamLegacy and the identity of Data Processors with whom it has been shared.
  • Right to Correction & Erasure (Section 12): Right to correct inaccurate data or request deletion of your account and personal data (subject to mandatory tax retention periods under Indian law).
  • Right of Grievance Redressal (Section 13): Right to have grievances addressed promptly by our Grievance Redressal Officer within 7 working days.
  • Right to Nominate (Section 14): Right to designate a nominee to exercise your rights in the event of death or incapacity.
